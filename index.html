Imagine it's the year 2087. Two billion people live underground in a huge network of cities called the Hollow. Things are breaking: power, water, air, medicine. This app is a control room that watches a (simulated) Hollow and helps people deal with each problem.

The numbers are made up by a simulation that updates every 2 seconds, so you can see the app react live. Each tab above is one area of life underground, and every box says what it does and what you can try.

🗺️ Map of the Hollow

Each square is a neighborhood. Level 1 is near the surface, Level 10 is the deepest. Click a square to see details.

OKWarningEmergency

👆 Click any square.

🚨 What needs attention right now

The system spots problems automatically and lists them here, worst first.

⚡ Blackout early warning

Blackouts last for days in the deep levels. The system watches how fast power use is climbing and warns people before the lights go out.

⚖️ Is power shared fairly?

Compares what each level needs with what a government that favors the upper levels would give it. Deep levels usually lose.

🌡️ Where is it too hot?

The deep levels get hotter every year. This shows which levels need cooling fans and repair crews the most.

💧 Unsafe drinking water

Sensors test the water in every neighborhood. If it's contaminated, the tap is locked so no one drinks it.

🚧 Flooded tunnels and safe routes

Sewage can flood tunnels and cut off whole neighborhoods. Pick two places and the app finds a path that avoids the flooding.


💬 Messages that work without the government network

The Directorate (the government) can shut down the network and read messages. Here, messages can also hop from neighbor to neighbor, like passing a note down a row of people.


🧪 Try it: send a message, then press the red "Simulate network shutdown" button at the top and send it again.

👨‍👩‍👧 Are my family members OK?

After a blackout, families on different levels can't reach each other. Tap one button to tell everyone you're safe, or that you need help.


🏠 Share space

Families are packed into tiny rooms. People with spare floor space can offer it, and people who need space can ask. The app matches the closest ones.


🫁 The real sickness numbers

Lung sickness is spreading, and the government's public numbers look too low. This compares the real cases (reported by residents) with the official ones.

💊 Where should medicine go?

Medicine is limited. This splits 1,000 medicine kits across levels, giving more to places where the most cases are being hidden.

🩺 Check your symptoms

The nearest clinic can be levels away. Tick what you feel and get basic advice right away.


😴 Sleep & mood tracker

Living without sunlight affects sleep and mood. Log how you feel, and it suggests light therapy if you need it.


🌱 Grow your own food

If the main farms cut off a neighborhood, it only has days of food. Enter your space and group size to see how much food you could grow.


🔒 Is the surface safe? (tamper-proof proof)

Sensors on the surface record air quality. Each reading is locked to the one before it, like links in a chain, so nobody (including the Directorate) can secretly change the data without it being noticed.

🧪 Try it: click "Simulate tampering" and watch the chain break. Then "Fix chain" to restore it.

🧑‍🚀 How should people go back up?

Going up will happen in stages. This compares three ways to do it and suggests who goes first.

🌍 How long would moving everyone take?

If the surface is safe, millions will want to go up. Enter how many, and see how long it could take.
